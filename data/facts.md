### Source dependency note

House vote-choice metrics are CIRCLE tabulations of **NORC Civic Health & Elections Project** data (snapshot labeled Nov 6 2026, 9 a.m. ET on the hub). Turnout estimates use **CIRCLE + SSRS Voter Poll**, not the NORC House vote-choice file. **AP VoteCast** appears in CIRCLE comparative text about the 2024 presidential race only — not as an independent second poll for the 2026 House rows on this page.
